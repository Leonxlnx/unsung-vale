# What changed

## 13 November 2026

Fixed

- Ground that could be seen through. Where the terrain stood higher than a court's paving
  it was cut away at the rim and one could look under it. The ground is now brought down to
  just under the stone round every court and at the head of the long stair, and the height
  maps were baked again.
- Stairs that could be seen through. Every step had been built without the face toward the
  foot of the flight, so the long stair was a ladder of treads from below.
- A shove could put the hero through a wall. Constructs overlapping him (a brute's rush
  above all) moved him with no regard for stone, and beside a pier that could leave him
  inside it. Every such push is now short and stops at a wall.
- Falling in a court no longer sends its fight back to the first wave.
- The map could be opened, and a string ridden, by a hero who had just fallen; he arrived
  with no life left and the screen could stay black. The dead open no menus now.
- Leaving for the title in the middle of a fight no longer carries a half-finished blow or
  conversation into the next session.

The Mute

- Three new kinds. The Seeker flies at the hero and bursts. The Toller sends a ring along
  the ground that has to be jumped or rolled through. The Shade lets go of its shape and
  takes it up again behind him.
- They work together. A chorister wards one of its own until the singer is broken, a brute
  that cannot reach the hero rushes along a line it marks on the ground, thralls cut twice,
  lancers of rank loose two bolts.
- Each named elite has a way of its own: marks where it strikes, breaks into seekers when
  it falls, cannot be hurt while its own still stand, wards two at once.
- Standard and Hard press harder. Gentle is as it was.

The blade

- The refrain mends wounds, a few times between places of rest.
- A bolt met with the blade goes back. A heavy blow on a staggered construct rings it.
- Cadence: blows landed without one taken build four steps of weight in the blade.
- Riposte: a blow slipped at the last instant opens the next cut.
- The last note: a named Mute or a giant that is nearly spent can be ended at a stroke.

The Vale

- Reliquaries on every mesa, each with a charm to wear; two at a time, three later.
- Chimes to strike from the lowest voice to the highest, song-crystals to break open.
- Echoes: once the Vale sings, a court will call its guard back at its spire.
- A trial won by striking bolts back. Five more quests. Dyes for the cape.
- People stroll, say a word in passing, call out during an echo, and each has something to
  say when the whole Vale sings. Birds over the spires, butterflies in the meadows.

The interface

- A Guide tab that keeps what the hint cards say. The journal lists every kind of Mute
  brought down and how much of the Vale has been seen to.
- Photo mode (H, or from the pause menu). Settings for camera shake, bright flashes and
  sprint as a switch. Tips while the valley loads.
- Hint cards sit in the lower left and leave the middle of the view clear.
